## 1. Purpose

FinNexus Solutions provides its employees, contractors, interns, and third-party personnel with access to information technology resources - including cloud-hosted systems, corporate devices, internal networks, SaaS applications, and communication tools - to enable the effective delivery of financial services to customers across India, the United States, and the European Union.

These resources exist to support FinNexus Solutions' business operations and must be used responsibly. Misuse - whether intentional or inadvertent - poses direct risks to the confidentiality of payment data and PII handled by FinNexus Solutions, the integrity of its financial transaction systems, and its standing under PCI DSS v4.0.1, DPDP Act 2023, and GDPR.

This policy defines the acceptable and prohibited boundaries of use for all FinNexus Solutions technology resources, sets clear expectations for conduct, and ensures that personnel understand their individual obligations in maintaining a secure operating environment.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- **All individuals** who access FinNexus Solutions technology resources, including permanent employees, fixed-term employees, contractors, interns, secondees, and authorized third-party vendor personnel, regardless of their location (Mumbai office, remote home-working, or client sites).
- **All FinNexus Solutions-owned or managed technology assets**, including but not limited to: company-issued laptops, mobile devices, cloud compute and storage resources (AWS/Azure/GCP), corporate email and collaboration tools (e.g., Slack, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365), internal applications, VPN infrastructure, and development tooling.
- **Personally owned devices** where those devices are used to access any FinNexus Solutions system, application, email, or data - whether through a corporate MDM profile, BYOD agreement, or informal access.
- **All network access** - including from FinNexus Solutions office networks, corporate VPN, public Wi-Fi, or home internet connections - when used to connect to FinNexus Solutions systems.

## 3. Policy Statements

### AU-01 - Authorized Business Use

FinNexus Solutions technology resources are provided primarily for conducting legitimate business activities in support of FinNexus Solutions' financial services operations. Personnel MUST use these resources in a manner that is consistent with their assigned role and the business activities for which they were engaged. Incidental personal use of company resources (e.g., brief personal web browsing during breaks) is permissible provided it: (a) does not interfere with business productivity; (b) does not violate any clause of this policy; (c) does not consume disproportionate bandwidth or storage; and (d) does not expose FinNexus Solutions systems to incremental security risk.

> **Framework Mapping:** ISO 27001:2022 A.5.10; NIST CSF GV.PO-02; PCI DSS v4.0.1 Req 12.3.2

---

### AU-02 - Prohibited Activities: Information Security and Data Integrity

The following activities are strictly prohibited on all FinNexus Solutions technology resources and will be treated as serious disciplinary matters:

- **a. Unauthorized data access or exfiltration:** Accessing, copying, modifying, or transmitting FinNexus Solutions data - including customer PII, payment account data, transaction records, or proprietary business information - without explicit authorization and a documented business need.
- **b. Circumventing security controls:** Disabling, bypassing, or tampering with endpoint security software (antivirus, EDR agents, DLP tools), network monitoring agents, MFA mechanisms, or any other control deployed on FinNexus Solutions systems.
- **c. Unauthorized software installation:** Installing software, browser extensions, scripts, or applications on company-issued devices without prior approval from the IT team. This includes AI-powered browser plugins, personal cloud sync clients, or remote-access tools not approved by IT.
- **d. Shadow IT:** Using personal or unapproved cloud storage (e.g., personal Google Drive, Dropbox) to store, sync, or share FinNexus Solutions data, including customer data, internal documents, or source code.
- **e. Credential sharing:** Sharing login credentials, passwords, one-time passcodes, or MFA devices with any other individual, including colleagues.
- **f. Malicious code:** Introducing, distributing, or executing malware, ransomware, spyware, cryptocurrency mining software, or any program designed to damage, disrupt, or compromise FinNexus Solutions systems.

> **Framework Mapping:** ISO 27001:2022 A.5.10, A.8.1; NIST CSF PR.AT-01, PR.DS-05; PCI DSS v4.0.1 Req 12.3; DPDPA Sec 8(5); GDPR Art 5, 32

---

### AU-03 - Prohibited Activities: Legal and Ethical Conduct

Personnel MUST NOT use FinNexus Solutions technology resources to:

- **a.** Access, store, transmit, or distribute content that is illegal under Indian law, EU law, or applicable US federal or state laws, including but not limited to: content that is obscene, defamatory, fraudulent, or in violation of intellectual property rights.
- **b.** Engage in or facilitate unauthorized financial transactions, market manipulation, money laundering, or any activity that conflicts with FinNexus Solutions' obligations as a regulated financial services provider under RBI guidelines.
- **c.** Conduct personal or third-party commercial activities unrelated to FinNexus Solutions' business without explicit written authorization from HR and the CISO.
- **d.** Harass, bully, defame, or discriminate against any individual - including colleagues, customers, regulators, or competitors - via any FinNexus Solutions communication channel.
- **e.** Misrepresent their identity or FinNexus Solutions' identity in any digital communication.

> **Framework Mapping:** ISO 27001:2022 A.5.10, A.6.2; NIST CSF GV.PO-02; PCI DSS v4.0.1 Req 12.1

---

### AU-04 - Internet and Web Browsing Standards

When accessing the internet using FinNexus Solutions-provided network connections or devices, personnel MUST adhere to the following:

- MUST NOT visit websites known to host malicious content, pirated software, or illegal material.
- MUST NOT download executable files, archives, or scripts from unverified or untrusted external sources onto company-issued devices.
- MUST use only FinNexus Solutions-approved and IT-managed browsers with endpoint security extensions enabled.
- MUST connect to FinNexus Solutions cloud and internal resources via the corporate VPN when working from non-office locations, in accordance with the Remote Access standards.
- SHOULD exercise caution on public Wi-Fi networks; MUST NOT access FinNexus Solutions production systems or process customer data over an unencrypted public Wi-Fi connection without the VPN being active.

> **Framework Mapping:** ISO 27001:2022 A.8.1, A.8.23; NIST CSF PR.PS-01; PCI DSS v4.0.1 Req 12.3

---

### AU-05 - Email and Collaboration Tool Standards

FinNexus Solutions provides email (corporate domain) and approved collaboration platforms (e.g., Slack, Google Workspace) for business communication. Personnel MUST:

- Use FinNexus Solutions corporate email accounts - not personal email accounts - for all business communications involving internal data, customer data, or regulated information.
- Exercise vigilance against phishing, spear-phishing, and social engineering attacks. Suspicious emails MUST be reported to IT Security via the designated phishing-reporting mechanism without clicking links or opening attachments.
- MUST NOT forward emails containing customer PII, payment account data, or confidential FinNexus Solutions documents to external personal email accounts.
- MUST NOT use collaboration tools (Slack, Teams, etc.) to share credentials, private keys, API keys, or any secrets - even in direct messages with colleagues.
- Be aware that all communications conducted via FinNexus Solutions corporate systems may be subject to monitoring and retention for audit, regulatory compliance, and legal purposes, as further described in AU-09.

> **Framework Mapping:** ISO 27001:2022 A.5.10, A.8.23; NIST CSF PR.AT-01; PCI DSS v4.0.1 Req 12.6; DPDPA Sec 8(5); GDPR Art 32

---

### AU-06 - Endpoint and Device Use Standards

Company-issued devices - including laptops, mobile devices, and any device enrolled in FinNexus Solutions' Mobile Device Management (MDM) system - are provided as tools for business productivity and remain the property of FinNexus Solutions at all times. Personnel MUST:

- Keep devices physically secure at all times; devices MUST NOT be left unattended in unsecured public spaces or unlocked in shared working areas.
- Ensure that the device screen locks automatically after no more than 5 minutes of inactivity, using a strong PIN or biometric authentication.
- Promptly report lost or stolen devices to IT Security - within 1 hour of discovery - to enable remote wipe and credential revocation.
- MUST NOT allow family members, friends, or other non-FinNexus Solutions individuals to use a company-issued device.
- Submit company-issued devices for IT inspection, patching, or security assessment upon request without undue delay.

Employees and contractors working under FinNexus Solutions' BYOD arrangement are subject to the separate BYOD Addendum and must accept MDM enrolment on any personally owned device used for FinNexus Solutions business.

> **Framework Mapping:** ISO 27001:2022 A.6.2, A.8.1; NIST CSF PR.PS-01; PCI DSS v4.0.1 Req 12.3

---

### AU-07 - Use of Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI Tools

Given FinNexus Solutions' high-risk data environment - spanning customer PII, payment account data, and regulated financial information - the use of publicly accessible Generative AI (GenAI) tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini consumer tier, Copilot free tier) and AI coding assistants is subject to the following restrictions:

- Personnel MUST NOT input, paste, upload, or otherwise transmit FinNexus Solutions customer data (PII, payment data), proprietary source code, internal credentials, strategic business documents, or legally privileged information into any public GenAI tool or consumer AI service.
- Use of AI tools for internal productivity tasks (e.g., drafting communications, summarizing internal-only documents) is permissible only using tools explicitly approved by the CISO and Legal/Compliance for FinNexus Solutions' data classification level.
- Engineering personnel using AI-assisted coding tools MUST ensure no secrets, API keys, or production credentials are present in any code submitted to an AI tool. AI-generated code that will be merged into production repositories MUST undergo standard security review as required by the Secure SDLC Policy.
- FinNexus Solutions will maintain an approved AI tools register, reviewed annually by the CISO and Legal/Compliance team.

> **Framework Mapping:** ISO 27001:2022 A.5.10, A.8.1; NIST CSF GV.PO-02, PR.DS-05; PCI DSS v4.0.1 Req 12.3; DPDPA Sec 6, 8; GDPR Art 5(1)(b), 25

---

### AU-08 - Use of Cloud Storage and External Data Transfer

FinNexus Solutions operates on public cloud infrastructure (AWS/Azure/GCP), and the movement of data within and out of these environments must be controlled. Personnel MUST:

- Use only FinNexus Solutions-sanctioned cloud storage buckets, drives, and repositories (as listed in the approved IT systems register) for storing and sharing business data.
- Ensure that any external sharing of FinNexus Solutions data with third-party vendors, regulators, or auditors is conducted through secure, encrypted channels (e.g., SFTP with mutual authentication, encrypted email, access-controlled secure file transfer portals) and only under a documented business authorization.
- MUST NOT make cloud storage buckets, object stores, or repositories containing FinNexus Solutions data publicly accessible - even temporarily - without explicit written sign-off from the IT Security Lead and CISO.
- Ensure that any bulk data export from FinNexus Solutions production systems is logged and subject to the Data Classification Policy requirements for the sensitivity of the data being moved.

> **Framework Mapping:** ISO 27001:2022 A.5.10, A.5.12; NIST CSF PR.DS-01, PR.DS-05; PCI DSS v4.0.1 Req 9.4; DPDPA Sec 8(5); GDPR Art 5(1)(f), 32

---

### AU-09 - Monitoring, Audit, and No Expectation of Privacy

Personnel using FinNexus Solutions technology resources should be aware that:

- FinNexus Solutions reserves the right to monitor, log, inspect, and audit activity conducted on its networks, systems, devices, and communication platforms. This includes internet usage, email content, file transfers, cloud access logs, and endpoint activity.
- Monitoring is conducted for purposes of security incident detection, regulatory compliance (PCI DSS, DPDPA, GDPR), legal hold obligations, and policy enforcement - and NOT for general surveillance of personal activities.
- Personnel have no expectation of personal privacy with respect to data stored on or transmitted through FinNexus Solutions-owned systems or corporate accounts.
- Any monitoring that intersects with personal data belonging to FinNexus Solutions employees will be governed by FinNexus Solutions' Employee Privacy Notice, maintained by HR and Legal/Compliance in compliance with DPDP Act 2023 consent and transparency requirements.
- The use of FinNexus Solutions systems constitutes implicit acknowledgement of and consent to this monitoring policy.

> **Framework Mapping:** ISO 27001:2022 A.8.16, A.5.10; NIST CSF DE.CM-03; PCI DSS v4.0.1 Req 10.1; DPDPA Sec 6 (Consent), 8(5); GDPR Art 5, 6(1)(f)

---

### AU-10 - Reporting Violations and Non-Retaliation

All FinNexus Solutions personnel have an obligation to report known or suspected violations of this policy - including their own inadvertent violations - promptly and in good faith. Reports MUST be directed to:

- The IT Security team (for technical violations and potential security incidents).
- Human Resources (for conduct-related violations).
- The CISO (for matters with potential regulatory or legal implications).

FinNexus Solutions operates a strict non-retaliation policy: no personnel who reports a genuine concern in good faith will face adverse employment action as a consequence of that report. Deliberate false reporting, however, is itself a policy violation. Individuals who suspect that a violation may constitute a personal data breach under DPDP Act 2023 or GDPR are directed to immediately contact the Legal and Compliance team to initiate breach notification assessment procedures.

> **Framework Mapping:** ISO 27001:2022 A.6.4, A.5.24; NIST CSF RS.CO-02; PCI DSS v4.0.1 Req 12.1; DPDPA Sec 8(6); GDPR Art 33

## 5. Enforcement and Sanctions

Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy are subject to a tiered disciplinary framework, applied in proportion to the severity, intent, and impact of the violation:

- **Tier 1 - Minor Breach** (e.g., brief personal use of non-approved streaming service on a work device; minor shadow IT usage with no data exposure): Verbal or written warning; mandatory completion of the Security Awareness Training module; note in personnel file.
- **Tier 2 - Moderate Breach** (e.g., forwarding internal documents to a personal email account; unauthorized installation of software with security risk; use of a public GenAI tool with non-sensitive internal content): Formal written warning; temporary restriction of access privileges; HR-led performance review; re-completion of full security awareness training.
- **Tier 3 - Severe Breach** (e.g., deliberate exfiltration of customer PII or payment data; wilful circumvention of security controls; introducing malware to FinNexus Solutions systems; unauthorized public disclosure of confidential data): Immediate suspension of all system access pending investigation; likely termination of employment or contract; potential civil and/or criminal referral under applicable Indian law (IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023), GDPR enforcement mechanisms, or PCI DSS incident reporting obligations.

All disciplinary actions under this policy MUST be documented by HR and reported to the CISO. Where a violation constitutes or may constitute a notifiable data breach under DPDP Act 2023 or GDPR, it MUST be simultaneously escalated to Legal and Compliance within the applicable notification window.
