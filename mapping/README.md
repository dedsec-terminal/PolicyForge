# Control Mapping Methodology & Maintenance

This directory maintains the mappings between FinNexus Solutions policies and applicable regulatory standards.

## Objective

The Control Traceability Matrix (CTM) ensures:
1. **Zero Gap Governance:** Every regulatory or industry requirement (ISO 27001:2022, NIST CSF 2.0, PCI DSS v4.0.1, DPDP Act 2023, GDPR) has an enforceable policy clause.
2. **Audit Readiness:** External and internal auditors can rapidly verify policy fulfillment against specific framework controls.
3. **Change Impact Analysis:** When a framework is updated or an internal process changes, impacted policies can be quickly identified.

## Maintenance Guidelines

- The Compliance team and CISO review the CTM bi-annually or whenever a new regulatory requirement or major policy revision is published.
- Traceability mappings should be updated whenever new policies are authored or existing policies undergo material modification.
